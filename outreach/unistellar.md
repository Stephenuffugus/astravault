## Body

Franck,

I'm Stephen Furpahs, Director of Astra Vault, a citizen-science astronomy app shipping in 2026. I wanted to reach out because Unistellar's eVscope is the closest thing to "real science from a backyard" that consumer astronomy has produced, and your work at SETI on automated asteroid occultation timing is exactly the kind of contribution we want to amplify with our app's users.

Astra Vault rewards every minute of genuine attention a user spends on real astronomy. Our users collect objects, log observations, and earn a token (SWS ATP) for every verified engagement — the more rigorous the observation, the higher the reward. We're not a planetarium app; we're a logbook + collection + citizen-science loop, designed to feed back to professional networks (CAMS, GMN, AAVSO, Globe at Night).

The specific ask: **read-only observation import from Unistellar's app/cloud into a user's Astra Vault.** When someone runs an eVscope session — a planetary capture, an asteroid occultation observation, a science campaign contribution — the observation lands automatically in their Vault, with full attribution to the Unistellar pipeline. We then surface their contribution to Astra Vault's Shared Sky aggregate ("3,217 observers tonight, including 142 Unistellar sessions").

This drives Unistellar adoption among Astra Vault users (every Vault user becomes a future eVscope candidate, especially in our higher tiers) and it gives existing Unistellar users a richer cross-platform logbook experience without changing your app.

I'd love 30 minutes to scope what this looks like on the integration side. I'm based in US Pacific but flexible to your schedule. If a Unistellar dev-relations contact is the better routing, please point me there.
