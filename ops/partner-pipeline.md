## Notes for the Director

- Never lead with the SWS Proof of Attention Protocol token economy in the
  first email. It's a footnote, not the pitch.
- Never ask for money in the first email. The first email opens the
  relationship. Money asks happen after value is established.
- Always end with a calendar offer. Vague "let's chat" gets ignored.
- Track replies daily. Even non-replies are data (which channels are dead).
