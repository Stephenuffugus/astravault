# SBIR Phase I Application — Scaffold + Narrative

Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) Phase I awards from NSF and NASA
fund early-stage innovation in qualifying companies. Astra Vault fits both.

## What this is

- **NSF SBIR Phase I:** $50,000-$305,000 for 6-12 month feasibility studies.
- **NASA SBIR Phase I:** $50,000-$150,000, 6-month feasibility, technical
  focus.
- **Free to apply.** Multi-month decision cycle.
- **Reverse-equity:** SBIR is non-dilutive. The money is a grant; you keep
  100% of the company. This is the BEST single funding source available
  to a solo LLC with IP.

## Eligibility

✅ Stephen's LLC qualifies (US-based, fewer than 500 employees, owned by
US citizens, innovation-stage).

⚠️ Phase I requires solid project narrative, not revenue or users. The
R&D reports in `research/` give us ~80% of the substantive content.

## Realistic timing

- **NSF SBIR submissions are accepted on a rolling basis** — submit when
  ready. Decisions take 3-4 months.
- **NASA SBIR has annual solicitations** — typically January and August
  release dates. Next window: check `https://sbir.nasa.gov/`.
- **Submission target:** 4-6 weeks of work to write a strong proposal.
  Submit by end of next month.

## What goes in the proposal

### Section A: Project summary (1 page)
Brief description of the innovation + the problem + the commercial vision.
Pull from `dealroom/one-pager.md`.

### Section B: Project narrative (10-15 pages)
The technical meat. Sections:
1. **Significance** — why this matters. Pull from
   `research/competitive-landscape.md` + `research/moment-capture-glasses.md`.
2. **Innovation** — what's novel. Pull from the patent claims (Patent #0
   issued + the 3 application patents drafted).
3. **Research plan** — what Phase I will demonstrate.
4. **Commercial vision** — Pull from `dealroom/pitch-deck.md` + the market
   size data in `research/competitive-landscape.md`.
5. **Team qualifications** — Stephen's background, the LLC's other work.
6. **Phase II vision** — what would follow if Phase I succeeds.

### Section C: References (page limit varies)
- ASCOM Alpaca spec
- Borovicka / Ceplecha / Vida triangulation papers
- 3DGS reconstruction papers
- The IAU Minor Planet Center
- Citizen-science citation: AAVSO, Globe at Night, Galaxy Zoo papers

### Section D: Budget (3-5 pages)
Standard structure. For Phase I at NSF:
- Stephen's salary (PI, ~40% effort): ~$60-90k
- Consultants / advisor fees: ~$10-20k
- Equipment: hardware testing (Seestar, Brilliant Labs Halo): ~$5k
- Travel: 1-2 conference presentations: ~$3k
- Indirect costs: typically 25-50% of direct (NSF caps)
- Total: $50-275k range

### Section E: Letters of support (optional but helps)
- Cloudy Nights / AstroBin admins
- GMN / SonotaCo network maintainers (if they reply to outreach)
- Any astronomer / scientist contacts

## The "Specific Aims" pitch (this is the most important paragraph)

**Aim 1:** Demonstrate the cross-glasses observation merge protocol (Patent
#1 application) on consumer smart glasses — Brilliant Labs Halo or Meta
Ray-Ban Gen 2 — combining multi-observer voice-triggered captures into a
triangulated 3D event reconstruction.

**Aim 2:** Validate the on-device plate-solve gating mechanism (Patent #4
application) against real sky observations from 100+ Astra Vault users,
demonstrating that attention-token issuance can be conditioned on
verified physical sky observation.

**Aim 3:** Build the citizen-science contribution pipeline: cross-reference
Astra Vault user observations against the Global Meteor Network (GMN) and
SonotaCo Network, publishing the contribution-back layer under CC BY 4.0
to broaden professional astronomy's observation base.

## Honest expected outcome

NSF SBIR Phase I awards are competitive. Realistic outcomes:

- **Probability of award on first submission: 15-25%** (typical first-time
  applicant success rate).
- **Probability of award if rejected first, resubmitted with reviewer
  feedback: ~40-60% on second try.**

The math: even at 20% odds × $150k average award = $30k expected value
from ~80-120 hours of writing work. That's ~$250-$375/hour effective rate,
non-dilutive, no equity given up. **Best ROI of any zero-budget
fundraising activity available to Stephen.**
