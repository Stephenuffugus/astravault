## The order of operations

If you want to sell or license, the order matters:

1. **File the 3 provisional patents.** Without them, the application-layer IP is unprotected and the asset is materially weaker. ~$1k filing fees + $5-15k attorney = the cheapest insurance you'll ever buy. Do this in the next 30 days.
2. **Polish the open-source alpaca-client and npm-publish.** Establishes that Astra Vault has shipped real engineering into the world. Free credibility.
3. **Use the one-pager to start 2-3 quiet conversations.** Don't pitch yet. Listen for which buyer profile is real.
4. **Then bring the deck.** Once a real buyer surfaces.
5. **Negotiate the retention shape AFTER they're interested**, not before. Asking "how much would you pay AND let me keep equity" up front is the wrong frame.

## Do not lead with

- The SWS Proof of Attention Protocol token economy. It's an asset, not a pitch. Mention it as a footnote after they're already interested in the astronomy product.
- The 11-patent candidate list. We narrowed to 3 + the existing one. Lead with what's defensible, not what's aspirational.
- Smart glasses adoption assumptions. They might not believe in glasses. The phone product has to stand on its own.
